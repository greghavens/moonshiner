# Harborlight volunteer applicant feedback summary

**Source label:** `[Volunteer Feedback]`

In June 2026, Harborlight sent a voluntary questionnaire to 124 people who had submitted an inquiry at Riverside Pantry or Northside Pantry during the six-month log period. Forty-seven people responded: 32 from Riverside and 15 from Northside.

Among the 47 respondents:

- 31 (66%) preferred a weekday evening orientation; 11 (23%) preferred Saturday morning, and five chose another time.
- 18 said they needed or strongly preferred the option to register by phone rather than solely through a web form.
- 13 reported that they had been unsure whether their application was complete or what would happen next.
- 10 wanted in-person help with the onboarding forms.

Open-text comments most often asked for a single contact person, a clear explanation of the first-shift process, and reminders that worked for people who did not regularly check email.

## Feedback limitations

The questionnaire was voluntary and nonprobability-based. Only 47 of 124 invited applicants responded, people with strong views may be overrepresented, and Riverside respondents made up more than two-thirds of the response group. The results describe these respondents and should not be presented as organization-wide prevalence estimates.
