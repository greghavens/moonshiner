# Draft administration note: weekly orientation pilot

**Source label:** `[Operations Note]`

The volunteer administration team outlined an eight-week, in-person pilot that can be run at either pantry site or at both sites. The design calls for one orientation session each week on Tuesday from 6:00 to 8:00 p.m. Each session is staffed by one volunteer coordinator and two peer mentors. Applicants may register through the existing web form or by phone with the volunteer office.

For a Riverside Pantry pilot, the team proposes these Week 8 targets:

- orientation attendance rate of at least 75%, compared with Riverside's recorded 64% baseline;
- at least 80% of orientation attendees completing a first shift within 14 days, compared with Riverside's recorded 70% baseline; and
- median application-processing time of two business days or less, compared with Riverside's recorded five-business-day baseline.

These Week 8 values are proposed administrative targets. Harborlight has not run this pilot, so they are not measured outcomes or forecasts of guaranteed performance.

## Administrative risks and proposed responses

- Separate web and phone registration streams can produce duplicate or omitted roster entries. The coordinator should maintain one shared session roster and reconcile both streams by noon each Monday.
- Incomplete application fields discovered at orientation can prolong processing. The coordinator should review the upcoming roster each Monday and contact applicants about missing fields before Tuesday's session.
- A late peer-mentor absence can leave check-in or first-shift coaching uncovered. The coordinator should confirm both assigned mentors 48 hours before each session and reassign a trained peer mentor from the current volunteer roster when needed.
- Circulating a full applicant roster can expose personal details unnecessarily. The coordinator should retain the master roster, give peer mentors only the session information they need, and use aggregate figures in the Week 8 report.
