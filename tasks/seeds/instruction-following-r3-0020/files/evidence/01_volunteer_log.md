# Harborlight Food Network volunteer activity log

**Source label:** `[Volunteer Log]`

**Period:** January 1–June 30, 2026

The volunteer office compiled these figures from site orientation sheets, the application tracker, and the shift-scheduling platform. Percentages below are rounded to the nearest whole percent.

| Recorded measure | Riverside Pantry | Northside Pantry |
|---|---:|---:|
| Completed volunteer inquiries | 96 | 84 |
| Attended a scheduled orientation | 61 of 96 (64%) | 63 of 84 (75%) |
| Orientation attendees completing a first shift within 14 days | 43 of 61 (70%) | 51 of 63 (81%) |
| Median time from complete application to processed onboarding record | 5 business days | 3 business days |

Riverside therefore recorded the lower orientation attendance rate, the lower 14-day first-shift completion rate, and the longer median application-processing time during this period. These are recorded administrative findings, not pilot results.

## Record limitations

- Site staff transcribed weekly orientation sheets by hand. Phone-in inquiry totals are missing for four of the 26 weeks, so the completed-inquiry denominator may be understated.
- The 14-day first-shift measure counts only shifts entered in Harborlight's scheduling platform. It does not capture informal help or shifts administered by partner organizations.
- The log follows volunteers only through their first recorded shift and cannot show longer-term retention.
