# Cordless vacuum comparison packet

## Scope and definitions

- This packet was assembled on 2026-07-15. Prices are manufacturer-listed purchase prices before tax on that date. They are a comparison snapshot, not live quotes.
- `sealed_filtration=yes` means the tested air path and filter housing were sealed. `anti_tangle=yes` means the supplied primary floor head has the maker's hair-release design. `user_replaceable_battery=yes` means the owner can remove and replace the battery without service.
- `wall_drilling_required=no` identifies charging storage that can stand on the floor without being fastened to a wall. A wall mount requires drilling. The lean-to charger does not require drilling, but its model must still pass every other requirement.
- Weight is the measured operating weight with the primary floor head and battery. Standard runtime was measured with that floor head at medium power. Bin capacity is the usable volume reported by the lab.

## Ratings

All rating fields use a 0–10 comparative lab scale, where higher is better. The noise score is already direction-adjusted, so a higher noise score means quieter measured operation. Runtime, handling, and dock are separate rating fields; the minute and capacity measurements are descriptive facts and are not substituted into the weighted formula.

The scores summarize one standardized test program. Home layouts, debris, battery aging, and maintenance can change real-world results, so the ratings are comparative lab scores rather than performance guarantees.

## Ownership-cost convention

`annual_filter_cost_usd` is the packet-date price of one complete replacement filter set. For this comparison, projected two-year ownership cost is purchase price plus two annual filter sets. No replacement battery is assumed during the two years. Sales tax, shipping, electricity, and any consumables not listed in the packet are excluded.

## Product observations

- Alder V9 is the lighter of the two sub-$400 models that satisfy all four non-price requirements. It posts the stronger hard-floor, handling, dock, filtration, and support scores of that pair, while having the smaller bin and shorter standard runtime.
- Cinder Flex S has the stronger low-pile, pet-hair, runtime, and noise scores of that qualifying sub-$400 pair, as well as the larger bin. Its counterweight floor dock folds the wand forward for storage but does not require drilling.
- Delta Pet Pro satisfies the non-price requirements but costs more than $400.00. Brio Slim+ requires wall drilling, Elm Air 7 lacks sealed filtration, and Fjord One lacks an anti-tangle floor head.
