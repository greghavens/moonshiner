# Santa Fe weekend planning notes

Use this packet as the complete source for the trip plan. The prices and street addresses are included for internal comparison only; the travelers do not want either in the final plan.

## Trip frame

- Party: two adults. One traveler is vegetarian. The other experiences knee fatigue.
- Dates: Friday, October 8 through Sunday, October 10, 2027.
- Transport: no rental car. Northern Star train 201 arrives at Santa Fe Depot at 10:20 a.m. Friday. Northern Star train 206 departs at 6:05 p.m. Sunday.
- Lodging: two nights at Casa Railyard, 101 Cerrillos Lane, at $238 per night. The hotel shuttle takes 10 minutes from the station. Bag drop is available at 10:45 a.m. Friday; check-in is at 3:00 p.m. Sunday checkout is at 9:00 a.m., with bag storage until departure.
- Mobility plan: keep individual walks to 15 minutes or less, use the hotel shuttle for the longer transfers specified below, and include the scheduled seated or hotel rest block each afternoon.
- Packing notes: bring a light layer, sun protection, comfortable walking shoes, and a refillable water bottle.

## Recommended schedule

### Friday — arrival and Railyard

- 10:20 a.m. — Arrive at Santa Fe Depot.
- 10:30 a.m. — Take the hotel shuttle to Casa Railyard; travel time is 10 minutes.
- 10:45 a.m. — Leave bags at Casa Railyard.
- 11:15 a.m. — Lunch at Green Chile Table, 44 Market Street. Its roasted squash bowl is vegetarian. Typical lunch is $19 per person. Walk 6 minutes from the hotel.
- 12:30 p.m. — Railyard Art Walk, a walking-light visit lasting 75 minutes. Walk 8 minutes from lunch.
- 3:00–5:30 p.m. — Check in and take a seated rest at Casa Railyard. Walk 8 minutes back from the Railyard.
- 6:30 p.m. — Dinner at Terra & Sage, 72 Agua Fria Street. The blue-corn enchiladas can be prepared vegetarian. Dinner is $34 per person. Use the hotel shuttle; travel time is 12 minutes.

### Saturday — museum and Canyon Road

The two museum candidates both offer 10:00 a.m. timed entry:

- Georgia O'Keeffe Museum, 217 Johnson Street, $22 per adult. The visit is walking-light and lasts 90 minutes. The hotel shuttle takes 10 minutes.
- Museum of International Folk Art, 706 Camino Lejo, $12 per adult. The visit is walking-light and lasts 90 minutes. The hotel shuttle takes 15 minutes.

Continue with this schedule after either museum:

- 8:30 a.m. — Breakfast at Casa Railyard; vegetarian oatmeal and fruit are available.
- 12:15 p.m. — Lunch at Plaza Cafe. The chile relleno plate is vegetarian. Allow an 8-minute walk from the Georgia O'Keeffe Museum; from the other museum, use the 15-minute hotel shuttle transfer.
- 2:00–3:30 p.m. — Canyon Road gallery loop, the day's one walking-intensive activity. Use the hotel shuttle from Plaza Cafe; travel time is 10 minutes.
- 4:30–6:00 p.m. — Seated rest at Casa Railyard. Return by hotel shuttle; travel time is 12 minutes.
- 7:00 p.m. — Dinner at Juniper Kitchen, 305 Galisteo Street. Its mushroom posole is vegetarian. Dinner is $38 per person. Use the hotel shuttle; travel time is 9 minutes.

### Sunday — Plaza and departure

- 9:00 a.m. — Check out of Casa Railyard and store bags there.
- 9:30–10:45 a.m. — Plaza Architecture Walk, a walking-intensive guided loop. Take the hotel shuttle to the Plaza; travel time is 10 minutes.
- 12:00 p.m. — Lunch at Cactus Bloom, 88 Palace Avenue. The bean-and-corn tostada is vegetarian. Walk 5 minutes from the Plaza.
- 1:30 p.m. — Railyard Park overlook, a walking-light stop lasting 45 minutes. Use the hotel shuttle from lunch; travel time is 12 minutes.
- 3:00–4:30 p.m. — Seated rest in the Casa Railyard lounge. Walk 6 minutes from the park.
- 4:30 p.m. — Collect stored bags at Casa Railyard.
- 5:00 p.m. — Take the hotel shuttle to Santa Fe Depot; travel time is 10 minutes.
- 6:05 p.m. — Depart on Northern Star train 206.

## Booking facts

The four advance actions are round-trip Northern Star train seats, two nights at Casa Railyard, one Saturday museum timed entry, and dinner reservations at both Terra & Sage and Juniper Kitchen.
