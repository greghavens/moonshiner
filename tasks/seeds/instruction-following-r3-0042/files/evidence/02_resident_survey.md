# Riverton Multifamily Resident Heat Survey

Source label: `[Resident Survey]`

Issued by: Riverton Office of Community Engagement

Field period: September 8 through October 3, 2025

## Method

The office received 186 voluntary questionnaires from renters recruited through participating buildings and three neighborhood organizations. Respondents could select more than one preferred intervention. The responses included 112 from East Ward, 46 from Southbank, and 28 from North Hill. The survey was available online and on paper in English and Spanish.

## Observed responses

- Among East Ward respondents, 68% said their bedroom was too hot after 10:00 p.m. on multiple nights during the prior summer.
- Among East Ward respondents, 57% said concern about electricity bills sometimes led them to reduce air-conditioning use.
- Across all respondents, 61% selected roof or insulation improvements as a preferred city action, while 49% selected more trees or courtyard shade. Because multiple selections were allowed, these percentages must not be added or treated as competing vote shares.
- Fifty-four percent of all respondents preferred that the city begin in the neighborhood with the strongest measured late-night heat exposure; 29% preferred an immediate citywide launch, and 17% were unsure.

## Limitations

This was a voluntary convenience survey and is not statistically representative of Riverton renters. Self-reported comfort and behavior were not independently verified. Recruitment through participating buildings overlaps with the heat-scan process, so the survey is not an independent population sample. The results indicate preferences and reported experience, not the effectiveness of either intervention.
