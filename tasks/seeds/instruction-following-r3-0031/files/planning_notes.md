# Porto planning notes

Use these fixed planning details for the itinerary. A place described as ticketed indoor may fill that day's one ticketed indoor slot. Other listed places do not.

## Baixa, Bolhao, and Ribeira

- Sao Bento Station: tiled main hall and station architecture; level access from the hotel base; free and indoors.
- Bolhao Market: local produce and food stalls; vegetarian lunch options; served by Bolhao metro station; covered and free to enter.
- Porto Cathedral terrace: architecture and a Douro view; reach the upper area by metro to Sao Bento followed by a short, carefully paced walk.
- Palacio da Bolsa: ticketed indoor guided visit in Ribeira.
- Ribeira promenade: level riverside walking and Douro views.
- Miradouro da Vitoria: outdoor Douro viewpoint; approach from the upper Baixa side to avoid a long uphill return.
- daTerra Baixa: named vegetarian restaurant for lunch or dinner.
- Mercado Ferreira Borges: covered market building that can replace an outdoor Ribeira stop in rain; free to enter.

## Cedofeita and Boavista

- Igreja do Carmo: azulejo-covered church exterior and architecture stop; free exterior viewing.
- Cordoaria Garden: level outdoor garden near Carmo.
- Casa da Musica: ticketed indoor architecture tour in Boavista.
- Rotunda da Boavista: outdoor architecture and neighborhood stop.
- Palacio de Cristal Gardens: free outdoor gardens with Douro views; use the level main paths.
- Bombarda galleries: free-to-enter small galleries suitable as a rainy substitution.
- Epoca Porto: named cafe with vegetarian lunch options in the Cedofeita area.
- Em Carne Viva: named vegetarian restaurant in Boavista.

## Vila Nova de Gaia

- Jardim do Morro: outdoor Douro and Porto skyline view beside Jardim do Morro metro station.
- Mosteiro da Serra do Pilar terrace: architecture and outdoor river view; use the upper metro approach rather than climbing from the riverfront.
- Planet Cork at WOW: ticketed indoor museum in the WOW cultural district.
- WOW covered courtyard: sheltered, free-to-enter public area that can replace an exposed riverfront stop in rain.
- Gaia riverfront: level outdoor promenade with Douro views.
- Cais de Gaia craft market: outdoor browsing along the level riverfront.
- Root & Vine: named restaurant in the WOW district with vegetarian lunch and dinner options.

## Connections and pacing

- Metro Line D links Sao Bento, Jardim do Morro, and General Torres. Trindade connections serve Bolhao and Casa da Musica.
- STCP bus 500 connects central Porto, Ribeira, Boavista, and the river corridor.
- Funicular dos Guindais avoids the steep climb between Ribeira and upper Baixa.
- Keep timed entries in chronological order. Start at or after 09:30 and schedule the final entry at or before 20:30.
- Walking notes should favor level or short routes. Public-transit notes may name metro, bus, or funicular; do not plan taxis, ride-shares, or private cars.
