# Riverton Office of Community Engagement: Late-Night Mobility Survey

**Field dates:** November 3–21, 2025  
**Source label for the committee brief:** `[Community Survey]`

The office collected 214 usable responses through employer break rooms, two neighborhood meetings, and an online questionnaire. Respondents could choose one preferred service model.

## Results

- 62% preferred an on-demand shuttle, 24% preferred a fixed-route extension, and 14% selected no preference.
- 71% said they would use a late-night service at least three times per week if it were fare-free.
- Nineteen respondents self-identified as having a mobility disability. Of those 19, 14 said a wheelchair-accessible vehicle was necessary and 11 said a phone-booking option was necessary. These needs overlap.
- Open-ended comments most often raised personal security while waiting, unpredictable pickup times, and lack of a non-smartphone booking channel.

## Limits

This was a voluntary, nonprobability survey. People with strong views may be overrepresented, and respondents were not independently verified as late-shift workers. Only 31 responses came from Eastbank, so neighborhood comparisons should not be made from this survey. Percentages are descriptive of respondents, not all Riverton residents or workers.
