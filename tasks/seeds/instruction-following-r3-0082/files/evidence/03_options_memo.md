# Riverton Transit Division: Late-Shift Service Options Memo

**Prepared:** January 12, 2026  
**Source label for the committee brief:** `[Options Memo]`

All ridership, wait-time, and cost figures below are planning projections rather than observed program results.

## Option A: six-month on-demand shuttle pilot

- Operate from 10:00 p.m. to 3:00 a.m. with two wheelchair-accessible vehicles.
- Accept bookings through a mobile app and a staffed phone line. Charge no fare during the pilot.
- A Northpoint-only service area is projected to cost $286,000 for six months and produce a median pickup wait of 18 minutes.
- A combined Northpoint-and-Eastbank service area is projected to cost $372,000 and produce a median pickup wait of 27 minutes with the same two vehicles.
- Projected Northpoint-only completed passenger trips are 650 in month 1, 900 in month 3, and 1,050 in month 6.
- Suggested month 6 performance targets for a Northpoint-only pilot are at least 1,000 completed passenger trips per month, a median pickup wait of 20 minutes or less, and 1.6 or fewer missed or shortened shifts per survey-panel worker per month. The pre-launch baselines are zero pilot trips, no applicable pilot pickup wait, and 2.1 missed or shortened shifts.

The principal operating risk is that peak demand could exceed two-vehicle capacity, increasing waits. The memo proposes weekly review of hourly demand and repositioning vehicles before adding service. A second risk is that app outages or low digital access could suppress bookings; the staffed phone line is the proposed alternative. The vendor would report only aggregated trip and wait-time data to the committee, with individual survey responses stored separately by the city research team.

## Option B: six-month fixed-route extension

- Extend Route 8 until 3:00 a.m. at 30-minute headways.
- Projected six-month operating cost is $410,000.
- The route would place 52% of sampled Northpoint late-shift endpoints within one-quarter mile of a stop.
- Projected completed passenger trips are 780 in month 6.

Option B offers a predictable path and schedule but reaches fewer sampled Northpoint endpoints than the on-demand service model. Neither option has been piloted in Riverton, so all option performance estimates remain uncertain.
