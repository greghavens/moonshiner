# Riverton Transit Division: Late-Shift Service Gap Scan

**Study period:** July 1–December 31, 2025  
**Source label for the committee brief:** `[Service Gap Scan]`

The Transit Division compared scheduled transit with geocoded late-shift endpoints in Northpoint and Eastbank. A "usable trip home" means a scheduled departure no more than 45 minutes after a shift ends between 10:00 p.m. and 2:00 a.m.

## Findings

- Northpoint has an estimated 720 workers ending shifts between 10:00 p.m. and 2:00 a.m. On a sample of 120 late-shift endpoints, 41% had a usable trip home on the existing bus schedule.
- Eastbank has an estimated 460 workers ending shifts in the same window. On a sample of 90 late-shift endpoints, 67% had a usable trip home.
- A convenience survey of 88 Northpoint late-shift workers found a mean of 2.1 missed or shortened shifts per worker per month attributed by respondents to transportation problems.
- Among those 88 respondents, 49 reported paying for a taxi or ride-hail trip at least twice during the study period because scheduled transit was unavailable.

## Interpretation and limits

The observed coverage gap is larger in Northpoint, but the scan does not establish that transportation availability caused missed or shortened shifts. The worker survey was a convenience sample recruited through five participating employers, and the endpoint samples do not cover every late-shift workplace. The six-month study period also excludes spring conditions. Results should not be treated as a citywide estimate.
