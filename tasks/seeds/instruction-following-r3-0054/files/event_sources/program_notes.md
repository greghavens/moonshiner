# Volunteer appreciation program notes

The approved program is three hours long. Keep these blocks in this order and preserve each duration when the event window changes.

| Duration | Activity | Lead | Operational note |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 20 minutes | Guest arrival and check-in | Welcome team | Place name badges at the west doors |
| 10 minutes | Welcome | Program director | Use the house microphone |
| 45 minutes | Buffet service | Catering captain | Release tables one at a time |
| 35 minutes | Volunteer stories | Story hosts | Introduce three confirmed speakers |
| 25 minutes | Recognition | Board chair | Present awards in surname order |
| 35 minutes | Community mingle | All staff | Keep the east alcove available for quiet seating |
| 10 minutes | Closing and departure | Program director | Direct departing guests to the west doors |

## Available room layouts and access setup

- Round-table option: six round tables with eight places at each table, for 48 places total.
- Cabaret option: eight half-round tables with six places at each table, for 48 places total.
- In the round-table option, at two center-aisle tables, make one of the eight places a chair-free wheelchair position; each of those tables therefore has seven chairs and one wheelchair position.
- Mark the table nearest the east alcove as quiet seating.
- Keep a clear center aisle from the elevator to the quiet seating and the front of the room.
- Put one large-print program at every place.
- Place wayfinding signs at the west doors and elevator.
- Test the house microphone and projection screen before doors open.
