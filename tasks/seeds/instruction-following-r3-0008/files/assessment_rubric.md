# Evidence literacy assessment rubric

Assess the plan through these four criteria, in this order.

1. **Question** — The learner frames one focused question about the selected community issue. The question is grounded in at least three observations and can be answered with evidence.
2. **Source judgment** — The learner evaluates authority, currency, relevance, and corroboration across at least three sources.
3. **Evidence reasoning** — The learner supports a claim with at least two data points and responds to one evidence-based counterclaim.
4. **Communication and reflection** — The learner completes the chosen capstone in its required length or extent and documents one revision prompted by the chosen feedback arrangement.

The capstone medium and feedback arrangement are learner choices. Apply the latest confirmed choices when turning these criteria into concrete checkpoints.
