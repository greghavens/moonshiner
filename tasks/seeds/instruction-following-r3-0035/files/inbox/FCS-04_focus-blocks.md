# FCS-04 — Phone placement and focus blocks

Type: personal observation

I logged 12 planned 50-minute focus blocks beginning at 09:30. Of nine blocks started with the phone in another room, seven were completed. Of three blocks started with the phone on the desk, one was completed.

The result is compatible with the notification audit's proposed batching plan, but the sample is uneven and block difficulty was not recorded. Phone placement and Do Not Disturb should therefore be treated as a testable routine, not as a proven cause of completion.

Reusable detail: the block log already has fields for start, completion, phone placement, and interruptions.
