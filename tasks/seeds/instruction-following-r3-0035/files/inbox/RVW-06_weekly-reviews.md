# RVW-06 — Sunday review pattern

Type: retrospective observation and template

Across six Sundays when I completed a review, I finished at least four of my five named weekly priorities in every following week. Across three Sundays when I skipped the review, that happened once.

The review template asks for experiment counts, a weekly median where relevant, one surprising observation, and one adjustment for the next week. The retrospective comparison is small, week difficulty varied, and the review may simply mark weeks when I already had more capacity.

Reusable detail: schedule the review at 18:00 and keep the three experiment measures separate before writing a short synthesis.
