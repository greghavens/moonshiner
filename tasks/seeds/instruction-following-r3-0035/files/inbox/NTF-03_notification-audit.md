# NTF-03 — Notification interruption audit

Type: personal observation and plan

During five weekdays, my phone produced a median of 63 non-call alerts per day. I timed 11 interruptions that occurred during planned work and needed a median of 18 minutes to feel fully resettled after each one.

The plan written in the note is to batch non-urgent notification checks at 12:30 and 17:00 and use Do Not Disturb during a protected work block. The audit cannot show that every alert caused the delay, and timing only 11 interruptions creates selection bias.

Reusable detail: record alerts encountered during the protected block and whether the block was completed.
