# MOV-05 — Midday walking and afternoon energy

Type: personal observation

On eight workdays with a 15-minute walk at 12:45, I recorded afternoon energy of at least 4/5 on six days. On five workdays without the walk, afternoon energy reached 4/5 on one day.

The walk could be a compact energy experiment that does not replace the primary sleep theme. The comparison is small and non-randomized, and lunch timing and morning workload were not controlled.

Reusable detail: log whether the walk happened and the 15:30 energy rating on the same 1–5 scale.
