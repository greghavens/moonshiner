# LGT-02 — Morning outdoor light trial

Type: personal observation

I took a 20-minute outdoor walk within one hour of waking on 10 mornings. I recorded alertness of at least 4/5 before 10:00 on seven of those mornings. On five mornings without the walk, alertness reached 4/5 once.

This note may connect morning light with the sleep-window note because both concern day-boundary cues. It remains an observational comparison: the days were not randomized, sleep duration varied, and weather may have affected both the walk and alertness.

Reusable detail: keep this as context for interpreting energy rather than adding a separate experiment during the first six-week cycle.
