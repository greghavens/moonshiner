# SLP-01 — Consistent sleep window

Type: personal observation

Over 14 nights, I recorded lights-out time, wake time, and next-day energy on a 1–5 scale. On the eight nights when lights-out was within 15 minutes of 23:00 and wake time was 07:00, next-day energy had a median of 4/5. Across the six irregular nights, next-day energy had a median of 2/5.

The pattern makes a consistent sleep window worth testing, but it does not establish causation. The sample is small, the ratings are subjective, and workload and caffeine were not controlled.

Reusable detail: a nightly log can mark whether lights-out fell inside 22:45–23:15, record wake time, and capture next-day energy.
