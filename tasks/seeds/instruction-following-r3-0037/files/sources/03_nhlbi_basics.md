# Source note: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute

Source label for the final handout: `[NHLBI]`

Primary page: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart/heart-beats

This protected note paraphrases NHLBI's basic explanation of the two blood pressure numbers.

Blood pressure is the force of blood pushing against artery walls as the heart pumps. A reading contains two numbers:

- **Systolic pressure** is the pressure while the heart's ventricles contract and pump blood out. Arterial pressure is highest during this phase.
- **Diastolic pressure** is the pressure between beats while the heart fills. Arterial pressure is lowest during this phase.

Blood pressure is conventionally written as the systolic number over the diastolic number. The unit `mm Hg` means millimeters of mercury.
