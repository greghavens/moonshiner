# Source note: CDC

Source label for the final handout: `[CDC]`

Primary page: https://www.cdc.gov/high-blood-pressure/measure/index.html

This protected note paraphrases the CDC checklist for obtaining a more accurate home blood pressure reading.

## Before measuring

- For the 30 minutes before a reading, avoid smoking, alcohol, caffeine, and exercise because these can raise the result.
- Empty the bladder before measuring.
- Sit quietly for at least five minutes in a comfortable chair.

## Position and technique

- Place the cuff directly on bare skin. It should be snug, but not excessively tight.
- Sit with the back supported, both feet flat on the floor, and legs uncrossed.
- Support the cuffed arm on a table at chest height rather than letting it hang at the side.
- Stay still and do not talk while the monitor is measuring.

Body position and recent activity can distort a reading. Accurate technique helps give the reader and health care team a clearer picture, but a home result by itself is not a diagnosis.
