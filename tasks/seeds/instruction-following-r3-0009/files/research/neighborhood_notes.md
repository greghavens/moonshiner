# Neighborhood field notes

Packet as of: 2026-08-12

- Ravenswood: Quieter residential blocks and direct rail choices are the recurring positives in the archived notes. Everyday groceries are within a short walk of the surveyed buildings.
- Lincoln Square: The notes emphasize independent shops, the library, and a calmer evening feel. Late-night options are less plentiful than in Uptown.
- Uptown: Lakefront access and frequent bus connections stand out. The evening survey also flags more late-night street noise on the Kenmore corridor.
- Albany Park: The notes call out grocery variety and value, alongside a longer downtown trip than the other north-side areas in this packet.
- Lake View: The lakefront and entertainment access score well, while the archived rush-hour trip is less direct to Clark/Lake.

These notes are qualitative observations from the archived packet, not additional selection rules.
