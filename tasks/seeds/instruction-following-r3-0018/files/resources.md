# Available workshop resources

The school has already assembled the following six-text packet:

1. **“Block by Block”** — an oral-history transcript in which a longtime resident describes changes to a neighborhood market street.
2. **“The Lot That Became a Garden”** — a local-news feature explaining how residents converted a vacant lot into a community garden.
3. **“Bus Stop at Dawn”** — a poem using sound and imagery to portray a neighborhood waking up.
4. **“Library Steps”** — a short photo essay with captions about how people use the public library entrance.
5. **“When the Creek Rose”** — a first-person narrative about neighbors responding to a flood.
6. **“Safe Crossings”** — excerpts from several residents’ public comments at a council meeting about pedestrian safety.

Available materials are printed copies of the packet, notebooks, pencils, sticky notes, highlighters, chart paper, markers, index cards, a projector, and a document camera. No other materials are available for this workshop.
