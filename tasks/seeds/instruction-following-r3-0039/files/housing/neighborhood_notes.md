# Neighborhood field notes

These notes were collected during weekday visits in July 2026. Transit figures in the CSV are the packet's modeled Tuesday 08:00 door-to-door trips; they are not guarantees. Grocery-walk figures are measured from each listed building. Availability, fees, utilities, and pet terms are listing snapshots and should be reconfirmed before signing.

## Riverbend note

- The river path is two blocks from Alder Loft and reaches a small off-leash area after a 14-minute walk.
- Weekend event traffic was noticeable near the main avenue during the visit.

## Beacon North note

- The full-service grocery is on a level route; the listing packet records a four-minute walk from Beacon Flats.
- Restaurants around Beacon Avenue stayed active after 22:00 during the visit, with audible street noise on the avenue side.

## South Quay note

- The community garden has a public gate beside Cedar Row; Linden Yard is three blocks farther inland.
- The walk back from Harbor Tech Campus includes an exposed waterfront segment with little shade.

## West Market note

- Daily shopping and the transit interchange cluster within four blocks of Dune Court.
- The building's shared laundry room has six machines for 28 units; no usage log was available.

## Old Harbor note

- Harbor Tech Campus is served without a transfer from the stop nearest both packet listings.
- Evening ferry horns were audible during the waterfront visit, but the packet has no indoor sound readings.

## Foundry District note

- A produce market is on the six-minute grocery route recorded for Foundry House.
- Freight trains were audible on eastern blocks during two evening visits; frequency and indoor noise were not measured.

## Juniper Hills note

- The return walk from the grocery and transit stops is steeply uphill for the final three blocks.
- The neighborhood visit found quieter side streets after 21:00, but no formal sound measurements were taken.
