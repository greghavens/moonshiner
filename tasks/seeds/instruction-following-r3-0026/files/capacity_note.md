# Holiday capacity note

Source label: `[Capacity Note]`

- The fulfillment center's standard planning capacity is 12,000 shipped units per week. This baseline is the observed average from the eight steady-state weeks ending 2026-09-20.
- Internal overtime can add at most 2,000 units in any week. Incremental overtime handling costs $3.10 per unit.
- The August overtime pilot processed 1,500 incremental units and observed 96.2% same-day completion. That was one pilot, not a holiday-period guarantee.
- The cost comparison includes overflow handling and any stated partner setup fee. It excludes returns, rework, and downstream freight because reliable estimates for those items are not available.
- Holiday demand figures are planning forecasts rather than booked orders. No actual holiday-period volume is available yet.
