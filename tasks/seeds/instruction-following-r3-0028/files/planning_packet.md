# GED Math planning packet

The bracketed IDs in this resource packet are citation labels for the facts below.

## [S1] Diagnostic snapshot

Jordan's latest 30-item diagnostic recorded 80% on number operations, 40% on algebraic reasoning, and 60% on data analysis. Within the item notes, signed-number operations and one-step equations accounted for six of the nine algebra errors. The snapshot does not report performance on geometry or provide an overall readiness score.

## [S2] Algebra Cards A

Algebra Cards A is a reusable set of 18 worked-example cards and 36 matching practice problems. Cards 1-6 cover signed-number operations, cards 7-12 cover one-step equations, and cards 13-18 cover two-step equations. An answer key is included. The cards can be viewed on a screen and do not require printing.

## [S3] Data Lab B

Data Lab B contains three 12-question sets: tables and graphs, mean and median, and simple probability. Each set includes an answer key and short error labels. The resource does not contain algebra instruction.

## [S4] Mixed Check C

Mixed Check C contains two parallel 20-question checks, Form 1 and Form 2. Each form has 10 algebra questions, 6 data-analysis questions, and 4 number-operations questions. The packet provides an answer key but does not provide a validated score conversion or a claim that either form predicts GED test performance.
