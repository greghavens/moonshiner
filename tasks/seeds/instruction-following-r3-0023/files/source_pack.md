# E-reader source pack

Use this pack as the sole factual source for the comparison. It is a pinned snapshot of first-party pages, not a promise that live prices or availability will remain unchanged. Cite facts with the source IDs shown below. A feature not established here must be described as unknown or not established, not assumed absent.

## [S1] Kobo Libra Colour — Rakuten Kobo U.S. product page

URL: https://us.kobobooks.com/products/kobo-libra-colour

Snapshot facts: U.S. listed device price $259.99; 7-inch E Ink Kaleido 3 color touchscreen; 32 GB storage; USB-C; 199.5 g; IPX8 (up to 60 minutes in up to 2 metres of water); ComfortLight PRO with adjustable brightness and colour temperature; physical page-turn buttons and left/right screen rotation. The page says ebooks can be borrowed directly from a local library through OverDrive and Libby integration, with OverDrive limited to select countries and ebook availability varying by library location. Its “up to 40 days” battery statement assumes 30 minutes of reading per day, front light at 30%, and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off. Actual size and weight may vary by configuration and manufacturing process.

## [S2] Kobo Clara Colour — Rakuten Kobo U.S. product page

URL: https://us.kobobooks.com/products/kobo-clara-colour

Snapshot facts: U.S. listed device price $179.99; 6-inch E Ink Kaleido 3 color touchscreen; 16 GB storage; USB-C; 174 g; IPX8 (up to 60 minutes in up to 2 metres of water); ComfortLight PRO with adjustable brightness and colour temperature. The page says ebooks can be borrowed through its OverDrive/Libby integration, subject to country and local-library availability. Its “up to 42 days” battery statement assumes 30 minutes of reading per day, front light at 30%, and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off. Actual size and weight may vary by configuration and manufacturing process. This source documents a touchscreen but does not establish whether the device has physical page-turn buttons.

## [S3] Kindle Paperwhite launch — Amazon

URL: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-kindle-color-scribe-paperwhite-entry

Snapshot facts: the base Kindle Paperwhite introduced in 2024 starts at $159.99 and has a 7-inch display. The announcement calls its battery life “up to three months” but does not give enough test conditions here to compare that claim directly with Kobo’s day estimates. The source describes touch interaction and does not establish physical page-turn buttons.

## [S4] Identify Your Kindle E-Reader — Amazon Customer Service

URL: https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GK33S847NN4V6Y83

Snapshot facts for Kindle Paperwhite (12th Generation), released in 2024: touchscreen; USB-C; adjustable warm light; up to 12 weeks of battery life; IPX8 waterproof rating; 16 GB internal storage, approximately 11.5 GB available to the user. This page does not state the usage assumptions behind the battery figure.

## [S5] Kindle 2024 specifications — Amazon Japan newsroom

URL: https://www.aboutamazon.jp/news/devices/kindle-2024

Snapshot fact: the base 2024 Kindle Paperwhite weighs 211 g. The page’s battery footnote bases “up to 12 weeks” on 30 minutes of reading daily, wireless off, and light level 13; results can vary with use.

## [S6] Reading Kindle Books — Libby Help

URL: https://help.libbyapp.com/en-us/6017.htm

Snapshot facts: U.S. libraries may offer Kindle-format loans through Libby. For a compatible loan, the borrower selects “Read With Kindle,” signs in to Amazon, and uses “Get Library Book” to deliver it. Kindle availability is U.S.-only, and not every book or library offers the Kindle format.
